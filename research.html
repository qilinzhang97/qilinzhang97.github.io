China Local Government Debt and Misallocation

Third-year paper · Advisors: Min Fang, Eugenio Rojas, Jonathan Adams

The rapid growth of local government debt in China has raised the risk of partial defaults among municipalities. The rising demand for refinancing by local governments crowds out private-sector resources, which leads to financial market instability and the misallocation of productivity.

Dynamic Aspect of Family Transfers under Gender Preference: Evidence from China

University of Wisconsin–Madison, 2021–2022 · Advisor: Christopher Taber

Builds a child-level panel from CHARLS to estimate how a child's gender affects parents' transfers, using family and child fixed effects to address selection on family and unobserved child characteristics.
