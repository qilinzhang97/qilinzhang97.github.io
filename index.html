Job market paper

Gender-biased Dynastic Savings and the Marriage Market: Evidence from China. Using household survey data, I document that the marriage market in China is assortative and that parents’ investment mix differs by child gender. In a dynastic model of parental investment and marriage matching, gender-biased investment lowers the marriage rate. As parents grow richer with China’s growth, the model explains 35 percent of the fall in the crude marriage rate between 2010 and 2024. All papers
