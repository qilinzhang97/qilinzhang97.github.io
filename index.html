Ph.D. student in economics at the University of Florida. I study macroeconomics and public economics, with a focus on China: how local government debt pulls credit away from private firms, and how families save and transfer wealth across generations.

Research

Local government debt in China has grown rapidly, and more municipalities now face the risk of partial default. In my third-year paper, I study how the resulting demand for refinancing crowds out private-sector borrowing, destabilizes financial markets, and leads to the misallocation of capital and productivity across firms.

With Min Fang and Yuchen Zhu, I also study gender-biased dynastic precautionary savings: how a preference for sons shapes how much Chinese households save and pass on to the next generation. Papers and details
